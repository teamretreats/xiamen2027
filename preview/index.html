⚠️ Common Tourist Scams

  1. Gulangyu ferry toutsClaim official tickets are sold out, then push pricey packages or unlicensed guides. Official tickets are only sold on the 厦门轮渡+ mini program.
  2. Seafood rip-offs"Recommended" stalls with rigged weighing, swapped seafood, hidden cooking fees, or prices per 两 instead of 斤.
  3. Cheap day-tour flyers"¥99, no shopping" tours that add fees on the bus and spend the day at shops.
  4. Boat trip touts (Huandao Road)Cheap sea trips with surprise surcharges and short, poor rides.
  5. Beach photo & flower trapsCostumed characters pose for photos or someone hands you roses, then asks for money.
  6. Nanputuo "blessings"Fake monks or "volunteers" offer free incense or fortune readings, then ask for big donations.
  7. E-bike rental trapsPrivate rentals invent damage to keep your deposit.
  8. Tea & specialty shopsTastings priced by weight, with the unit price only revealed at the till, plus fake dried goods.
  9. Taxi detoursChatty drivers take the long way or steer you to shops that pay them commission.
  10. Street photographersOne price is quoted, then they charge per person afterwards.
